COMPOUND INTENT V2 — BENCHMARK PREPARATION
Decision: PROVISIONAL. V2 implementation NOT authorized.
Stage: TELCHI / INVESTIGATION / benchmark quality gate. No runtime, SQL,
Foundation, SAP, WhatsApp, server or additional LLM corpus run.

1. Purpose
V1 remains rejected by the RCA. The RCA showed the 86 structural regressions are
not 86 proven semantic errors and that two exactness predicates (12 vs 10)
disagreed. This stage delivers a defensible measurement contract before any V2,
without re-running the 114-turn LLM battery: it only re-scores the frozen run01
artifacts.

2. Deliverables
- Gold schema and annotation guide (gold_schema.py, annotation_guide.md).
- Annotated seed of 10 known cases (seed_gold.py): 8 simple controls + Abraham
  001/002. Request-aware, not accepted-state-only.
- Sealed holdout of 20 new turns across 5 conversations (holdout.py,
  holdout_cases.json, holdout_manifest.json).
- Deterministic layered evaluator and tests (evaluate.py, metrics.py,
  canonicalize.py).
- Documented 12/10 resolution (metrics.py, README.md).
- Stable baseline metrics recomputed from run01 (baseline_metrics.json).
- Objective V2 authorization gates (authorization.py, v2_passive_contract.md).

3. Measurement contract
Four layers scored separately: source_ledger (literal), request_extraction
(raw + canonical), accepted_state, execution_eligibility.
Named predicates: RECALL_COMPLETE, SET_EXACT (primary), and the legacy
reproductions LEGACY_RECALL_FAMILY_EXACT (12) and LEGACY_GOLD_KEYS_EXACT (10).
Representation equivalences are declared in canonicalize.py and applied
symmetrically: end->end_exclusive, sort dict->list, eq->in, OR-of-eq -> in.
Nothing else is normalized.

4. 12 vs 10 resolution
run.summarize filters candidate atoms to gold-populated field families, so facets
added in gold-empty families are invisible (12). evaluate.raw_score restricts by
gold top-level keys, so added facets in empty-but-present fields are visible
(10). The two disagreeing turns are TCS_003_T02 and TCS_020_T01.
12 answers "did it lose a requirement"; 10 answers "did it lose or invent one".
Both are published; SET_EXACT is primary.

7. Authorization
authorization.authorize() returns INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE unless gold is CERTIFIED
and the baseline is frozen; otherwise it returns AUTHORIZE only if all
behavioural gates pass (100% literal preservation, zero new authorizations, zero
new blocks, unchanged accepted state, no measurement regression, zero assertion
certification, zero extra LLM calls, overhead p95 <= 50 ms and <= 2%).
